# Logo assets

`sovr.png` (512×512, 193 KB — under the registry's 250 KB logo limit) and
`sovr.svg` — the SOVR chain/token mark for the Cosmos
Chain Registry submission: the hexagon tile from the official Sovren Blockchain
logo lockup, extracted from the brand vector source (selected by the brand
owner, 2026-08-11; PNG re-rendered at 512×512 from the final brand artwork 2026-08-18 —
same mark, higher resolution, byte-identical to `sovr-networks/images/sovr.png`). Referenced by `logo_URIs`/`images` in `chain.json` and
`assetlist.json` (mainnet), with the testnet records pointing at the mainnet
images via `image_sync` — standard registry practice.

URLs in the JSON files are the canonical post-merge chain-registry paths
(`raw.githubusercontent.com/cosmos/chain-registry/master/sovr/images/…`); they
resolve once the upstream submission merges.
