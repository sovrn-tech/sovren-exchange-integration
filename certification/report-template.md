# Sovren Technical Integration Certification Report

> **Scope — technical integration only.** Certifies the enumerated native
> SOVR technical behaviors (node sync/failover, chain-ID/network config,
> address generation, deposit detection, failed-tx rejection, duplicate-credit
> and duplicate-withdrawal prevention, withdrawal construction/signing,
> sequence management/recovery, sweep/fee handling, scanner-restart recovery,
> balance reconciliation, a controlled testnet round trip, and offline
> validation of the shipped mainnet network manifest). Does **NOT**
> certify the exchange's AML/sanctions program, legal/regulatory compliance,
> cybersecurity controls, private-key management, custody policy,
> solvency/insurance, customer ledger, or withdrawal-limit/fraud controls.
